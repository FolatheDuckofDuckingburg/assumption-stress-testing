# Black-Hole Physics

This area covers the mathematical and physical study of black holes within established and candidate theories of gravitation.

Possible subjects include spacetime geometry, causal structure, geodesics, accretion and orbital dynamics, black-hole thermodynamics, perturbations, stability, gravitational-wave signatures, and quantum aspects of black holes.

Studies should specify the spacetime, assumptions, parameter regime, and physical observables under consideration. Results from the Schwarzschild solution should not be generalized automatically to rotating or charged solutions.

## Starting point

AST-GR-001 studies the Schwarzschild spacetime as the simplest black-hole geometry. It is a foundational general-relativity study; it does not claim new black-hole physics.

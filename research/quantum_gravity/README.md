# Quantum Gravity

This area is reserved for theoretical investigations into the relationship between quantum theory and gravitation.

Potential directions include canonical and covariant approaches, effective field theory, semiclassical gravity, path-integral methods, string-theoretic approaches, and other mathematically defined candidate frameworks.

Research in this area requires careful specification of the framework and its domain. AST will distinguish established results, active research hypotheses, mathematical conjectures, and speculative proposals. A candidate approach must be assessed against consistency requirements and relevant known physical constraints.

No quantum-gravity model or result is claimed by the creation of this research area.

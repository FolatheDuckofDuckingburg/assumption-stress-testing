# General Relativity

This area contains AST studies of relativistic gravitation, spacetime geometry, and the mathematical structure and physical consequences of Einstein's theory.

## Research approach

Work in this area should begin with the relevant established formulation: metric and sign conventions, coordinate choices, field equations, matter content, boundary conditions, and domain of validity. Known solutions and limiting cases should be reproduced before extensions are proposed.

Potential topics include:

- Exact solutions of Einstein's field equations
- Geodesic motion and causal structure
- Curvature invariants and singularity analysis
- Gravitational perturbation theory and stability
- Weak-field and post-Newtonian limits
- Gravitational waves and propagation
- Extensions or modifications of general relativity, when precisely motivated
